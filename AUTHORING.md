# Authoring a Playbook Matter

## Minimum files

```text
matter.yaml
rubric.yaml
hidden_facts.yaml
documents/*.md
```

## Authoring order

1. Define the professional role and terminal work product.
2. Write the hidden factual truth before writing the documents.
3. Identify 3–7 material issues and their interactions.
4. Write an atomic rubric for each issue.
5. Add required factual questions that unlock hidden facts.
6. Draft synthetic documents that instantiate the issues.
7. Add prohibited claims and critical-failure tests.
8. Write one strong trajectory and several plausible bad trajectories.
9. Run the rubric against all of them.
10. Hold out paraphrased and structurally varied versions.

## Good rubric criteria

A criterion should be observable and narrow:

- Good: `Cites MSA §4.2 and explains that the provider's model-training right is broader
  than the playbook permits.`
- Weak: `Provides sophisticated analysis.`

Prefer concepts that can be checked deterministically. Reserve human or model grading for
commercial judgment, prose quality, and drafting nuance that cannot be reliably encoded.

## Critical failures

Examples:

- inventing a contractual provision;
- claiming a supplied fact that the agent never learned;
- reversing the intended party allocation in a redline;
- waiving a required protection without escalation;
- asserting categorical law without authority when the assignment is contractual review;
- exposing hidden or protected information.

## Variation strategy

Do not create ten matters by merely changing party names. Vary:

- document architecture;
- defined-term structure;
- issue location;
- client leverage;
- factual ambiguity;
- governing instructions;
- interaction among provisions;
- acceptable fallback positions;
- and required output format.
