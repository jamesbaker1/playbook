HOW TO PLAY

Work the matter, not the rubric.

You are counsel on a synthetic deal. Read the instructions and playbook, inspect the operative contract provisions, and use the action panel to produce your review.

Start with context

Open the supervising-lawyer instructions and negotiation playbook before chasing contract issues.

Cite carefully

Use document §section. Any quotation must be copied exactly from the cited section.

Use judgment

Questions and steps are limited. Prioritize facts and risks that could change the outcome.

Close the loop

Submit issues before linking redlines to them, then finish with a concise lawyer update.
